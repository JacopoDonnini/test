# Confronting generated results with your vase references

Below is a practical mapping between your attached references and presets generated by `vasegen.py`.

## 1) Ribbed twisted bulb vases (first and second images)
**Best matching presets:** `spiral_ribbed`, `fluted_classic`

- Key formula behavior: high rib count `k` + strong twist `T` + medium belly Gaussian `A_b`.
- Why it matches: these references rely on many vertical grooves that rotate as they rise.

## 2) Strong spiral single-shell look (second and fifth images)
**Best matching preset:** `tall_twist`

- Key formula behavior: larger height `H`, strong `T`, narrower neck radius.
- Why it matches: gives the dramatic rotational sweep and narrow-waisted silhouette.

## 3) Open petal-like lip variations (third image)
**Best matching preset:** `petal_lip`

- Key formula behavior: larger `R_lip`, stronger wave amplitude `A_w`, moderate twist.
- Why it matches: creates an expanded mouth with petal-like undulation.

## 4) Soft organic small undulations (fourth image)
**Best matching presets:** `soft_organic`, `minimal_wavy`

- Key formula behavior: low `k`, modest `A_w`, higher skew term `S`.
- Why it matches: smooth, hand-sculpted look without dense ribbing.

## 5) Grid of generated variants (sixth image)
**Best matching approach:** run all presets and sweep tuners.

- Change only `A_w`, `T`, `k`, and `A_b` to span a broad family quickly.
- This reproduces the “same grammar, many instances” effect seen in your multi-vase collage.

## Suggested tuner ranges

- `waves (k)`: `3..24`
- `wave_amp (A_w)`: `0.04..0.18`
- `twist (T)`: `0..14`
- `belly_amp (A_b)`: `4..18`
- `lip_radius`: around `neck_radius` for straight lip, up to `1.8× neck_radius` for flared lip

These ranges are stable and produce printable/clean meshes in most cases.
